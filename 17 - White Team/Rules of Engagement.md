# Rules of Engagement (ROE)
The contract that makes testing legal and safe.
- **Scope:** exact in-scope IPs/domains/apps; explicit exclusions.
- **Authorization:** signed written permission (get-out-of-jail).
- **Timing/windows**, allowed techniques, data handling rules.
- **Emergency contacts** + stop conditions (production impact).
- Reporting requirements + evidence handling.
No ROE = no testing. This is the #1 legal/ethical control.
