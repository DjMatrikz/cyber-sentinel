# White Team Overview
The **referees/organizers** of an exercise (esp. red vs blue engagements).
- Define scope, rules of engagement (ROE), objectives, and timeline.
- Observe, score, and arbitrate; ensure safety and fairness.
- Hold the "out of bounds" list and the emergency stop.
- Neutral party — not attacking (red) or defending (blue).
Common in formal cyber ranges, competitions, and large exercises.
