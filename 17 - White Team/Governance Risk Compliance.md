# Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)
- **Frameworks:** NIST CSF, ISO 27001, CIS Controls, SOC 2.
- **Regulations:** PCI-DSS (cards), HIPAA (health), GDPR/CCPA (privacy).
- **Risk management:** identify -> assess (likelihood x impact) -> treat (mitigate/accept/transfer/avoid).
- **Policies/standards/procedures** hierarchy; audits; evidence.
- Ties to the Security+ "Program Management & Oversight" domain and IT PM work.
