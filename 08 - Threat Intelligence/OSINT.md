# OSINT (Open-Source Intelligence)
Public info gathering.
- **People/org:** LinkedIn, theHarvester, hunter.io (emails), social media.
- **Infra:** whois, DNS, crt.sh (cert transparency), Shodan/Censys (exposed devices), Wayback Machine.
- **Google dorking:** `site:`, `filetype:`, `intitle:`, `inurl:`.
- **Maltego** for link analysis.
Great for recon phase + defending (know your own exposure).
