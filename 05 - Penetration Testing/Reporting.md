# Pentest Reporting
The deliverable that actually matters to clients.
- **Executive summary** — business risk, no jargon.
- **Findings** — each with: description, severity (CVSS), affected assets, evidence/screenshots, reproduction steps, remediation.
- **Methodology & scope** — what/when/how.
- Prioritize by risk. Clear remediation = value.
Tools: Serpico, Dradis, or a clean template. This skill separates juniors from pros.
