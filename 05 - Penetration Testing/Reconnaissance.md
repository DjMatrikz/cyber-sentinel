# Reconnaissance
- **Passive** (no touch): OSINT, whois, DNS, Google dorking, Shodan, LinkedIn, certificate transparency.
- **Active:** ping sweeps, port scans, banner grabbing.
Tools: `whois`, `dig`, `theHarvester`, `recon-ng`, `amass`, Shodan, Maltego.
`nmap -sn 10.0.0.0/24` for live hosts. See [[Nmap]], [[OSINT]].
