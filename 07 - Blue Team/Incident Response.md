# Incident Response
**NIST IR lifecycle:**
1. **Preparation** — tooling, runbooks, contacts.
2. **Detection & Analysis** — validate, scope, triage severity.
3. **Containment** — short-term (isolate host) + long-term.
4. **Eradication** — remove malware/persistence, patch.
5. **Recovery** — restore, monitor.
6. **Lessons Learned** — postmortem.
Preserve evidence (chain of custody). Document timeline. See [[Digital Forensics]].
