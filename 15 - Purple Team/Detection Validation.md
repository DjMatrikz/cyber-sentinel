# Detection Validation
Proving your detections actually work.
- Run the attack -> confirm the log/alert appears -> measure time-to-detect.
- Document: technique, expected telemetry, actual result, gap, fix.
- Feeds [[Detection Engineering]] (write/tune Sigma rules).
- Continuous: re-test after changes; track ATT&CK heatmap coverage over time.
