# Command & Control (C2)
How red teams control compromised hosts. **Authorized engagements only.**
- Frameworks: Cobalt Strike (commercial), Sliver, Mythic, Havoc, Metasploit.
- **Concepts:** beacons/implants, callback intervals, redirectors, domain fronting, encrypted channels.
- **Evasion:** sleep/jitter, process injection, signed binaries (LOLBins), AMSI/ETW bypass.
- **Blue side:** detect beaconing (regular callbacks), unusual processes, C2 IOCs.
