# Topologies & Cabling

## Topologies
- **Star** — all nodes to a central switch (most common LAN). One cable fails = one node down.
- **Mesh** — every node to every node (redundant, used in WAN/wireless). Full vs partial.
- **Bus** — single backbone (legacy). One break = whole network down.
- **Ring** — each node to two others (token ring, legacy).
- **Hybrid** — mix of the above.

## Copper cabling
| Category | Speed | Max distance |
|----------|-------|--------------|
| Cat5e | 1 Gbps | 100 m |
| Cat6 | 10 Gbps (to 55 m) / 1 Gbps (100 m) | 100 m |
| Cat6a | 10 Gbps | 100 m |
| Cat7/Cat8 | 25–40 Gbps | 30 m (Cat8) |

- **T568A vs T568B** — wiring standards. Same on both ends = straight-through; A/B mix = crossover.
- **Coax** — RG-6 (cable/satellite).

## Fiber
- **Single-mode (SMF)** — long distance, laser, yellow jacket.
- **Multi-mode (MMF)** — shorter distance, LED, aqua/orange jacket.
- Connectors: LC, SC, ST, MTP/MPO.
