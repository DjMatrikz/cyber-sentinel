# Wi-Fi Fundamentals
- **Bands:** 2.4 GHz (range, channels 1/6/11), 5 GHz (speed), 6 GHz (Wi-Fi 6E).
- **Standards:** 802.11 a/b/g/n(Wi-Fi4)/ac(Wi-Fi5)/ax(Wi-Fi6/6E).
- **SSID** (network name), **BSSID** (AP MAC), **channel**, **beacon frames** advertise APs.
- **Frame types:** Management (beacon, probe, auth, deauth), Control, Data.
- **Monitor mode** — capture all frames without joining (needs capable adapter, e.g. Alfa).
