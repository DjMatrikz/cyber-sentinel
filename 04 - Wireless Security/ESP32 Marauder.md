# ESP32 Marauder
ESP32-based Wi-Fi/Bluetooth testing firmware.
- Wi-Fi: scan APs/clients, beacon spam, deauth (where legal), probe sniffing, PCAP capture.
- Bluetooth: scan/sniff BLE.
- Runs on boards like the WROOM/dev kits, or integrated devices.
- Output PCAPs -> analyze in Wireshark.
**Deauth and jamming are illegal on networks you don't own — lab only.**
