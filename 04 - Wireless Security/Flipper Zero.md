# Flipper Zero
Multi-tool for Sub-GHz, RFID/NFC, Infrared, GPIO, BadUSB.
- **Sub-GHz (300-928 MHz):** capture/replay (garage, some remotes) — research legal limits.
- **RFID 125kHz / NFC 13.56MHz:** read/emulate access cards (test your own).
- **BadUSB:** acts as HID keyboard -> runs payloads (Rubber Ducky scripts).
- **Infrared:** universal remote.
Firmware: official vs community (Unleashed/RogueMaster). **Use only on what you own/are authorized to test.**
