# Interview Preparation
- **Technical:** OSI model, TCP vs UDP, common ports, CIA triad, how HTTPS works, explain a recent CVE, walk a pentest methodology.
- **Scenario:** "You see failed logons from a foreign IP — what do you do?"
- **Behavioral (STAR):** lean on your ops leadership stories.
- **Home lab talk** — interviewers love genuine hands-on curiosity. Your projects are the differentiator.
